Dear Manu,

I don’t think I say this nearly enough, so I wanted to make a small space on the internet just for you—to say thank you.

And on the days you feel lonely, remember that you are never truly alone. Just as you have always been there for me, I am right here in your corner—rooting for you, appreciating you, and grateful every single day that you exist.

With all my gratitude and warmth,
Shelyna
